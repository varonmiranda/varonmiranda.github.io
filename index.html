01 · About

Operator first. Analyst second. Builder on weekends.

I'm a mechanical engineer who spent five years inside automotive supply chains at Tata Motors and Mahindra & Mahindra, across India and Finland. I was promoted four times, from graduate trainee to Senior Manager, running supplier networks, launches, and recovery plans when things broke.

Somewhere along the way I built Mahindra's first real-time logistics incident dashboard, and realized the best operations decisions were the ones the data made obvious early. That's why I'm at American University's Kogod School of Business, studying Business Analytics and Finance, and why I'm moving into management consulting.

On the side I build software for the problems I lived: Hektor for supply chain planning and DealBridge for M&A deal teams.
